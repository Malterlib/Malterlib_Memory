#### Serviced allocation sizes

Small allocations are served through special sub slabs that keep track of allocations with doubly linked indices that index within the sub slab. 1 byte allocations have uint8 indices while the other sizes use uint16 indices.

Each sub slab of small allocations contains a header that contributes to the overhead, and in some cases internal fragmentation. The header is 56 bytes for 1 byte allocations and 24 bytes for the other sizes.
